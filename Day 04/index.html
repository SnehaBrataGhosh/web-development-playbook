Defination lists(Description Lists)

Unordered Lists
Used for collections of items where the order does not matter. Items are typically marked with bullets (discs, circles, or squares) by default.
Ordered Lists
Used for sequences of items where the order is important (e.g., steps in a process or a ranking). Items are typically marked with numbers, letters, or Roman numerals by default.
Description Lists
Also known as definition lists, these are used to organize items in a format similar to a dictionary, consisting of term-description pairs.
